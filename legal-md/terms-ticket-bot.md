> **Draft.** These terms are a first version for review and may change before Robtic Ticket opens to everyone.

These terms cover adding and using **Robtic Ticket**, Robtic's support-ticket bot (ticket panels, forms, staff controls and transcripts), and its dashboard at robtic.org/discord/public. They add to the [Robtic Discord Terms](/terms/discord) and the [Robtic Terms](/terms/robtic), which cover your Robtic account. What the bot keeps, and why, is in [Robtic Ticket Privacy](/privacy/ticket-bot).

## 1. Who can add and configure it

- Only a server **Administrator** can add the bot or open its dashboard for a server.
- While Robtic Ticket is not open to everyone, it only works in servers on its allowlist.
- To use the dashboard you need a Robtic account with your Discord account linked; see [Account Privacy](/privacy/account).

## 2. What the bot does

Robtic Ticket opens a private channel between a member and your staff when the member picks an option on a ticket panel. Each server sets its panels, the questions a member answers before opening, who can see and act on a ticket, the staff controls (claim, close, reopen, delete, add or remove members, transfer, rename), where logs and transcripts are sent, and which roles may not open tickets.

## 3. Decisions about people

Your server's staff decide how each ticket is handled, who is blocked from opening tickets, and when a member is called back to a ticket. **Your server's administrators are responsible for these decisions and for treating members fairly.** Robtic does not review or take part in them.

## 4. Your responsibilities

- Use the bot within [Discord's Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms) and Community Guidelines.
- Don't ask members for passwords, payment details or other sensitive data in ticket forms.
- Tell your members that ticket conversations are saved as transcripts your staff can read.
- Don't use the bot (including its direct-message "come to your ticket" notice) to spam or harass anyone, or to break the [acceptable use rules](/terms/robtic#3-acceptable-use).

## 5. Availability

We work to keep the bot online, but can't promise it will always be available or that no data is ever lost. Keep your own copy of transcripts that matter.

## 6. Removing the bot

You can remove the bot at any time. Your server's settings, tickets and transcripts are kept for 30 days in case you add it back, then deleted (see [How long we keep it](/privacy/ticket-bot#how-long-we-keep-it)).

## 7. Changes

We may change these terms. For important changes we will tell server administrators in the dashboard before they apply.
