This page explains what **Robtic Ticket** keeps about the servers it is in, their staff and their members, why, and what you can do about it. Using the bot follows the [Robtic Ticket Terms](/terms/ticket-bot); your Robtic account itself is covered in [Account Privacy](/privacy/account).

## What we collect

- **Server settings** chosen by administrators: panels and their options, form questions, messages, permissions, staff and blocked roles, and the log and transcript channels.
- **Tickets:** who opened each ticket, which option, its number and channel, who claimed it, who was added or removed, when it was opened, closed or deleted, and the close reason if one was given.
- **Form answers:** what a member entered in the ticket's form, including any files they uploaded.
- **Transcripts:** the messages sent in a ticket channel (text, embeds and links to attachments) with each author's name and avatar, saved when the ticket is closed or deleted.
- **Ids:** Discord user, server, channel and role ids, needed to link all of the above.

## Why we use it

- To open, run and close tickets the way the server set them up.
- To keep transcripts the server's staff can read and search later (by who opened or claimed a ticket, its kind or date).
- To show administrators their tickets and statistics in the dashboard.

## Who can see it

- A member sees their own ticket while they have access to it.
- The server's staff and administrators see tickets, form answers and transcripts, as the server's permissions allow. Anyone who can read the log or transcript channel sees what is posted there.
- Robtic's administrators, only when needed to run the bot, fix a problem you ask about, or deal with abuse.

## How long we keep it

- Tickets and transcripts: as long as the bot is in the server, unless the server deletes them sooner.
- When the bot is removed, the server's settings, tickets and transcripts are kept for 30 days in case it is added back, then deleted.

## Your choices

- Administrators can turn off forms, transcripts and logs, which stops collecting for them.
- Members can ask the server's administrators, or us, for a copy of what is kept about them, or to remove it from a server.

## Changes

If this page changes in an important way, we will tell server administrators in the dashboard before it applies.
