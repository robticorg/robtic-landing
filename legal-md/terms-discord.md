These terms cover **Robtic Discord**: Robtic's Discord bots (Robtic System, Robtic Staff, Robtic Ticket, Robtic Voice and Robtic Guard), their dashboard at robtic.org/discord/public, and plugins. You accept them when you first turn Robtic Discord on. They add to the [Robtic Terms](/terms/robtic), which cover your Robtic account. Each bot also has its own terms and privacy page, linked from the bot's page.

## 1. Turning it on

- Robtic Discord is turned on once per Robtic account, by accepting these terms.
- To manage a server you also link your Discord account. We only read which servers you are in and which you manage.
- Only a server **Administrator** can add a bot or open the dashboard for that server.

## 2. What you get

Ready-made bots you add to your servers and configure from the dashboard: levels and points, staff management, tickets, voice, protection, logs and more. Some bots or features work only in servers on an allowlist while they are not open to everyone. Features can be switched on or off per server and may change as the bots grow.

## 3. Your responsibilities

- You decide how the bots are configured in your server and are responsible for using them within [Discord's Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms) and Community Guidelines.
- Tell your members what the bots record in your server (for example activity for levels, or ticket transcripts).
- Don't use the bots to harass members, punish people unfairly, or break the [acceptable use rules](/terms/robtic#3-acceptable-use).

## 4. Plugins

- Plugins you make (with blocks or with Robtic Rules code) are yours. A plugin runs only in the servers that install it, with the permissions of the bot it runs on.
- If you share a plugin, other people can see its blocks or code, clone it and install it. Don't share plugins that contain secrets, or that do anything these terms forbid.
- We may remove or turn off a plugin that breaks these terms, harms servers, or overloads the bots.
- Robtic's official plugins are public: anyone can read their code.

## 5. Points and RC

Points and RC have no cash value and can't be sold or exchanged outside Robtic. We may correct balances that were earned through bugs, spam or abuse.

## 6. Availability

We work to keep the bots online, but can't promise they will always be available or that no data is ever lost.

## 7. Removing a bot

You can remove a bot from your server at any time. What it keeps afterwards, and for how long, is in that bot's privacy page.

## 8. Changes

We may change these terms. For important changes we will tell you in the dashboard before they apply, and may ask you to accept them again.
