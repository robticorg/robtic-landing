> **Draft.** These terms are a first version for review and may change before Robtic Staff opens to everyone.

These terms cover adding and using **Robtic Staff**, Robtic's staff-management bot (ranks, staff points, warnings, reports, appeals, vacations and applications), and its dashboard at robtic.org/discord/public. They add to the [Hosting Terms](/terms/hosting), which cover your Robtic account. What the bot keeps, and why, is in [Robtic Staff Privacy](/privacy/staff-bot).

## 1. Who can add and configure it

- Only a server **Administrator** can add the bot or open its dashboard for a server.
- While Robtic Staff is not open to everyone, it only works in servers on its allowlist.
- To use the dashboard you need a Robtic account with your Discord account linked; see [Account Privacy](/privacy/account).

## 2. What the bot does

Robtic Staff runs a staff team: a numbered rank ladder taken from your role order, staff points and weekly promotion lists, staff and member warnings, reports handled in private threads, appeals, punishments with evidence and approvals, vacations and staff applications. Each server sets its own roles, channels and rules.

## 3. Decisions about people

The bot carries out the rules your server sets (for example: three staff warnings demote one rank, or a missing identity check pauses staff roles). **Your server's administrators are responsible for these decisions and for treating staff and members fairly.** Robtic does not review or take part in them.

## 4. Your responsibilities

- Use the bot within [Discord's Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms) and Community Guidelines.
- Only attach evidence you have the right to share, and only what a decision needs.
- Tell your staff that the bot records their activity, points and warnings.
- Don't use the bot to harass anyone or break the [acceptable use rules](/terms/hosting#4-acceptable-use).

## 5. Availability

We work to keep the bot online, but can't promise it will always be available or that no data is ever lost. Keep your own records of decisions that matter.

## 6. Removing the bot

You can remove the bot at any time. Your server's settings and staff records are kept for 30 days in case you add it back, then deleted (see [How long we keep it](/privacy/staff-bot#how-long-we-keep-it)).

## 7. Changes

We may change these terms. For important changes we will tell server administrators in the dashboard before they apply.
