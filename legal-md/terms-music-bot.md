These terms cover adding and using **Robtic Music**, Robtic's music bots for voice channels, and their dashboard at robtic.org/discord/public. They add to the [Hosting Terms](/terms/hosting), which cover your Robtic account. What the bots keep, and why, is in [Robtic Music Privacy](/privacy/music-bot).

## 1. Who can add and configure it

- Only a server **Administrator** can add the music bots or open their dashboard for a server. Robtic Music runs alongside Robtic System, which must be in the server.
- While Robtic Music is not open to everyone, it only works in servers on its allowlist.
- To use the dashboard you need a Robtic account with your Discord account linked; see [Account Privacy](/privacy/account).

## 2. What the bots do

Each server can have several music bots. Each one stays in its own voice channel or moves to wherever music is asked for, plays what members request, and keeps a queue with a control panel. Each server sets its bots, channels, who may control them and their profiles.

## 3. What is played

Members choose what is played. **Your server is responsible for what its members play** and for having the right to play it. Robtic does not choose, host or check the music, and may stop playback that breaks these terms or the law.

## 4. Your responsibilities

- Use the bots within [Discord's Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms) and Community Guidelines, and the terms of the services music is played from.
- Don't use the bots to broadcast content you have no right to share, to disturb voice channels, or to break the [acceptable use rules](/terms/hosting#4-acceptable-use).

## 5. Availability

We work to keep the bots online, but can't promise they will always be available, that every source can be played, or that playback never stops.

## 6. Removing the bots

You can remove the bots at any time. Your server's settings are kept for 30 days in case you add them back, then deleted (see [How long we keep it](/privacy/music-bot#how-long-we-keep-it)).

## 7. Changes

We may change these terms. For important changes we will tell server administrators in the dashboard before they apply.
