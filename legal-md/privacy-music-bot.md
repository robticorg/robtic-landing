This page explains what **Robtic Voice** keeps about the servers it is in and their members, why, and what you can do about it. Using the bots follows the [Robtic Voice Terms](/terms/music-bot); your Robtic account itself is covered in [Account Privacy](/privacy/account).

## What we collect

- **Server settings** chosen by administrators: the server's music bots, their voice channels, panel channel, who may control them, and each bot's profile.
- **The queue:** what is playing and queued in each bot, and who asked for each song, while the bot is playing.
- **Ids:** Discord user, server, channel and role ids, needed to link the above.

The bots join voice channels to play audio. **They do not record or keep what anyone says in a voice channel.**

## Why we use it

- To play music the way the server set it up, and to show the queue and who requested each song.
- To show administrators their music bots in the dashboard.

## Who can see it

- Members in the server see the queue and the panel where the server shows them.
- The server's administrators see the settings in the dashboard.
- Robtic's administrators, only when needed to run the bots, fix a problem you ask about, or deal with abuse.

## How long we keep it

- The queue: only while music is playing; it is cleared when the bot stops or leaves.
- When the bots are removed, the server's settings are kept for 30 days in case they are added back, then deleted.

## Your choices

- Administrators can remove a music bot or change who may control it at any time.
- Members can ask the server's administrators, or us, for a copy of what is kept about them, or to remove it from a server.

## Changes

If this page changes in an important way, we will tell server administrators in the dashboard before it applies.
