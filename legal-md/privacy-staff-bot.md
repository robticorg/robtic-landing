This page explains what **Robtic Staff** keeps about the servers it is in, their staff and their members, why, and what you can do about it. Using the bot follows the [Robtic Staff Terms](/terms/staff-bot); your Robtic account itself is covered in [Account Privacy](/privacy/account).

## What we collect

- **Server settings** chosen by administrators: which roles and channels mean what, point values, and the server's rules (identity check, applications, vacations).
- **Staff records:** rank, status (active, on vacation, removed), points and their history, actions taken (tickets and reports claimed, warnings given), and the history of promotions, demotions, transfers and removals.
- **Warnings and punishments:** for staff and members, with the reason, who gave it, and the evidence attached.
- **Reports and appeals:** what was reported, the conversation between the reporter and the staff member handling it, the decision and its evidence. The reporter's identity is shown only to the staff handling the report.
- **Applications:** the answers an applicant gives (name, age, city, department), and for transfers the screenshots they upload.
- **Ids:** Discord user, server, channel and role ids, needed to link all of the above.

## Why we use it

- To run the staff systems the server turned on, and to keep the history decisions depend on (for example, appeals and repeated warnings).
- To give each staff member fair points for their work.
- To show administrators their staff team in the dashboard.

## Who can see it

- Staff see their own points, rank and warnings.
- The server's administrators and the managers they choose see their staff team, reports, appeals and applications, as each feature allows.
- A reporter's identity: only the staff handling that report and administrators.
- Robtic's administrators, only when needed to run the bot, fix a problem you ask about, or deal with abuse.

## How long we keep it

- Staff records, warnings and punishments: as long as the bot is in the server. Warnings are never deleted on removal, only marked removed, so history stays honest.
- Applications: until the application is decided, then as part of the staff record if accepted.
- When the bot is removed, the server's settings and records are kept for 30 days in case it is added back, then deleted.

## Your choices

- Administrators can switch systems off, which stops collecting for them.
- Staff and members can ask the server's administrators, or us, for a copy of what is kept about them, or to remove it from a server.

## Changes

If this page changes in an important way, we will tell server administrators in the dashboard before it applies.
