These terms add to the [Hosting Terms](/terms/hosting) for teams and organisations on Robtic, and for people invited into someone else's workspace.

## 1. The owner

- The person who buys a Team plan, or accepts an organisation's offer, owns the team.
- The owner is responsible for the team's plan and for what its people do with its workspaces and apps.
- The owner can hand the team to another of its people; the old owner stays in the team as an admin.
- The owner cannot leave the team without handing it over first.

## 2. Roles

- **Owner** and **Admins** are in every workspace of the team, add and remove people, and decide who reaches which workspace.
- **Managers** see the team's people and what they did.
- **Members** work in the workspaces they are given.
- Only the owner and admins make workspaces in a team, up to the number the plan allows.

## 3. Resources

A team has a pool of memory, CPU and storage set by its plan or offer. The owner and admins share it out between the team's workspaces, and the apps in a workspace share what it gets.

## 4. Inviting people

- People can be invited into a workspace to work on its apps, within the number the plan allows.
- Whoever invites someone chooses what they can do and which apps they see, and is responsible for that choice.
- An invited person must follow the Hosting Terms like any other user.

## 5. Leaving and removal

- Anyone but the owner can leave a team at any time; the owner and admins can remove people.
- Someone who leaves loses access to the team's workspaces. Workspaces they own in the team leave with them and go back to their own plan.
- People in a team keep their own personal plan for their own workspaces; it is separate from the team's.

## 6. When a team's plan ends

The team's workspaces and apps are kept for a while, but apps cannot be started or created until the owner renews the plan. We may remove a team's apps long after its plan ended, after telling the owner by email.

## 7. Working together

People in a team can see each other on the workspace canvas and in the activity log. Be fair to each other: do not use your access to harm the team's apps or data.
