> **Draft.** These terms are a first version for review and may change before Robtic System opens to everyone.

These terms cover adding and using **Robtic System**, Robtic's community bot (levels, points, streaks, moderation and logs), and its dashboard at robtic.org/discord/public. They add to the [Hosting Terms](/terms/hosting), which cover your Robtic account. What the bot keeps, and why, is in [Robtic System Privacy](/privacy/system-bot).

## 1. Who can add and configure it

- Only a server **Administrator** can add the bot or open its dashboard for a server.
- While Robtic System is not open to everyone, it only works in servers on its allowlist. Adding it to another server does nothing, and it leaves.
- To use the dashboard you need a Robtic account with your Discord account linked; see [Account Privacy](/privacy/account).

## 2. What the bot does

Robtic System gives members XP and levels for chat and voice, activity points that can be converted to RC, daily streaks, conversation combos and leaderboards. It can moderate with evidence (jail, mute, warn), run shortcuts and post logs to the channels you choose. Features can be switched on or off per server and may change as the bot grows.

## 3. Your responsibilities

- You decide how the bot is configured in your server and are responsible for using it within [Discord's Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms) and Community Guidelines.
- Tell your members that the bot records activity (messages counted, voice time, streaks) for levels and points.
- Don't use the bot to harass members, to punish people unfairly, or to break the [acceptable use rules](/terms/hosting#4-acceptable-use).

## 4. Points and RC

Points and RC have no cash value and can't be sold or exchanged outside Robtic. We may correct balances that were earned through bugs, spam or abuse. Rates are set by each server.

## 5. Availability

We work to keep the bot online, but can't promise it will always be available or that no data is ever lost. Leaderboards and statistics may be reset if they become inaccurate.

## 6. Removing the bot

You can remove the bot from your server at any time. Its settings for your server are kept for 30 days in case you add it back, then deleted (see [How long we keep it](/privacy/system-bot#how-long-we-keep-it)).

## 7. Changes

We may change these terms. For important changes we will tell server administrators in the dashboard before they apply.
