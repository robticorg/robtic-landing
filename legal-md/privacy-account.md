This page explains what Robtic keeps about you and your account, why, and what you can do about it. What we keep about the apps you host is in [Hosting Privacy](/privacy/hosting). We do not sell your data and there are no ads.

## What we collect

- Your username, email address and password (stored only as a hash), your picture if you add one, and your two-step verification settings.
- If you sign in with or connect **GitHub** or **Discord**: your id, username and email there. For GitHub, also an access token (kept encrypted) so Robtic can clone, deploy and push your repositories.
- Your plan, its dates, your subscription history, and your teams and roles.
- The devices you are signed in on: the browser and system, the IP address and when it was last seen.
- An activity log of actions on your account, apps, workspaces and teams, with the IP address. People who run a team or workspace see the activity in it.

## Why we use it

- To sign you in safely and show you where your account is signed in.
- To send emails you need: confirming your email, new sign-ins, invitations and plan reminders.
- To keep Robtic secure: finding abuse and helping you see who did what.
- To handle plans and, once card payments open, payments through Stripe (Robtic does not store card numbers).

## Cookies

Robtic uses only the cookies needed to keep you signed in and to protect forms. The panel also keeps a few settings in your browser (such as the team you last chose) and caches its own files so it loads faster. There are no tracking or advertising cookies.

## How long we keep it

- Your account details, for as long as you have the account.
- Device records for 30 days after a device was last seen; activity logs for as long as they help with security and support.
- When you delete your account, its data is removed, except what we must keep by law.

## Your choices

- See and change your details under Account; sign out other devices under Account → Devices.
- Disconnect GitHub or Discord at any time.
- Ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete your account.

## Children

Robtic is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

If this page changes in an important way, we will tell you by email or in the panel before it applies.
