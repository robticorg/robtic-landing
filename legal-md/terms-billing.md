These terms explain how paying for Robtic works: plans, renewals, refunds, cards and invoices. They are part of the [Hosting Terms](/terms/hosting). Questions about a payment: billing@robtic.org.

## 1. The free month

- Every account gets one free month of Robtic Hosting, with no card needed. It starts when you turn on Robtic Hosting, not when you make your account.
- During the free month you can try every feature it includes. When it ends without a payment, the plan has ended: your apps and files are kept, but apps can't be started or created until you choose a plan.

## 2. Plans renew every month

- Paid plans are monthly subscriptions. They **renew automatically** at the end of each month, charged to the card saved for the subscription, at the price shown when you subscribed.
- **We email you 3 days before every renewal** with the plan, the amount and the date, so you can cancel or change your card in time.
- You can cancel at any time in Settings → Plan. The plan keeps running until the end of the month you already paid for, then ends. Nothing more is charged. Until then you can resume it.

## 3. Changing plans

- **Moving up** applies at once. You pay the difference for the rest of the current month right away, and the bigger plan starts once that payment goes through.
- **Moving down** applies at your next renewal, so you keep what you paid for until then. Your apps must fit in the smaller plan.

## 4. Refunds

- If you pay for a plan **while you are still on your free month**, the free month ends that day and your paid month starts. That first payment can be refunded if you ask within **7 days** of paying, from Settings → Plan. This is offered once per account.
- If you pay **after your free month has ended**, that payment is not refundable.
- **Renewals are never refunded.**
- An approved refund returns every payment of that subscription (the first payment and any upgrade difference in those 7 days) and ends the plan. Refunds reach your card in 5 to 10 days.

## 5. Failed payments

- If a renewal can't be charged, we email you and try again over the next days (up to 7). Your plan keeps running meanwhile.
- If the payment still fails, the subscription ends and so does the plan.

## 6. Cards

- Card payments are processed by Stripe. Your card number never reaches Robtic: we keep only the card's brand, last 4 digits, expiry date and the billing address you give.
- You can save cards and pick one when you pay. The default card pays your renewals.
- A card that pays a running subscription can only be removed after that subscription is cancelled. Removing a card asks for your two-step code, or your password, or a code sent to your email.

## 7. Invoices and tax

- After every successful payment we email you an invoice (PDF) from billing@robtic.org. Your invoices are also in Settings → Plan.
- Where tax applies, the invoice shows it, and checkout shows whether it is included in the price or added to it before you pay.

## 8. Payment links

- Robtic may send you a payment link for something agreed with you (for example a custom setup). It shows what it is for and the amount before you pay, works without signing in, and you get an invoice by email.
- A link meant for one payment closes once it is paid. If two people pay it at the same moment, the second payment is refunded automatically.

## 9. Changes

We may change prices or these terms. A new price applies to new subscriptions; we tell you by email before a change reaches a subscription you already have.
