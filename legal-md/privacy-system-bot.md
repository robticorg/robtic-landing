This page explains what **Robtic System** keeps about the servers it is in and their members, why, and what you can do about it. Using the bot follows the [Robtic System Terms](/terms/system-bot); your Robtic account itself is covered in [Account Privacy](/privacy/account).

## What we collect

- **Server settings** chosen by administrators: channels, roles, rates, rewards, shortcuts and log channels.
- **Activity, per member and server:** how many messages counted (not their content), XP and level, voice time, streaks, combo scores, points and RC balances and their history.
- **Moderation records:** punishments, their reasons, the moderator, and the evidence attached.
- **Ids:** Discord user, server, channel and role ids, needed to link all of the above.

The bot does **not** store the text of members' messages. It reads a message only to count it and to run commands and shortcuts.

## Why we use it

- To run the features a server turned on: levels, points, streaks, leaderboards, moderation and logs.
- To keep balances fair: finding spam, abuse and bugs.
- To show administrators their server's statistics in the dashboard.

## Who can see it

- A member sees their own profile, balance and rank, and public leaderboards.
- The server's administrators and staff see their server's statistics, moderation records and logs.
- Robtic's administrators, only when needed to run the bot, fix a problem you ask about, or deal with abuse.

## How long we keep it

- Activity and balances: as long as the bot is in the server.
- Moderation records: as long as the server keeps the bot, so appeals and history stay possible.
- When the bot is removed, the server's settings and data are kept for 30 days in case it is added back, then deleted.

## Your choices

- Administrators can switch features off, which stops collecting for them.
- Members can ask the server's administrators, or us, to remove their data from a server.
- Ask us for a copy of what the bot keeps about you.

## Changes

If this page changes in an important way, we will tell server administrators in the dashboard before it applies.
