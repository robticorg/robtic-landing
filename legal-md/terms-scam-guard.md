> **Draft.** These terms are a first version for review and may change.

These terms cover adding and using **Scam Guard**, Robtic's bot that stops scam raids, and its dashboard at robtic.org/discord/public. They add to the [Robtic Discord Terms](/terms/discord) and the [Robtic Terms](/terms/robtic), which cover your Robtic account. What the bot keeps, and why, is in [Scam Guard Privacy](/privacy/scam-guard).

## 1. Who can add and configure it

- Only a server **Administrator** can add the bot or open its dashboard for a server.
- To use the dashboard you need a Robtic account with your Discord account linked; see [Account Privacy](/privacy/account).

## 2. What the bot does

Scam Guard reads the messages posted in your server's channels to spot a scam raid: one member posting the same image in several channels within a short time. When that happens it can delete every copy, time the member out and post an alert for your moderators. Each server chooses how many channels and how many seconds count as a raid, which of these actions happen, how long a timeout lasts, and which channels and roles are left alone.

## 3. Automatic actions

Scam Guard acts on its own, without a person checking first. It can be wrong, for example when a member shares the same picture in several channels on purpose. **Your server's administrators are responsible for its settings and for undoing an action that was a mistake** (removing a timeout, for example). Robtic does not review these actions.

## 4. Your responsibilities

- Use the bot within [Discord's Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms) and Community Guidelines.
- Give the bot only the permissions it needs: read messages, manage messages, time out members and send messages in the alert channel.
- Don't use the bot to harass members or to break the [acceptable use rules](/terms/robtic#3-acceptable-use).

## 5. Availability

We work to keep the bot online, but can't promise it will always be available or that it will catch every raid.

## 6. Removing the bot

You can remove the bot at any time. Your server's settings and its list of stopped raids are kept for 30 days in case you add it back, then deleted.

## 7. Changes

We may change these terms. For important changes we will tell server administrators in the dashboard before they apply.
